Privacy Policy — Compass One

Last updated: 31 July 2026

Compass One is built to work without knowing who you are. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no user profiles, and it does not collect or transmit personal information. This page explains exactly what the app touches and why.

The short version. The compass works entirely offline. Location is optional and never leaves your device. Saved places stay on your phone. There is no analytics and no crash reporting of any kind.

Who we are

Compass One is published by GWAPPS. For anything in this policy, write to chennoufo11@gmail.com.

Sensors

The compass reads your device's rotation vector, magnetometer, accelerometer and gyroscope. These readings are processed on your device, in real time, to draw the compass and calculate the Trust Score. They are never stored and never transmitted.

Location

Location is optional. The app does not ask for it on first launch, and the compass works fully without it. It is requested only when you use a feature that needs it:

  • The GPS screen, to show your coordinates, altitude, speed and accuracy
  • True North mode, to apply the magnetic declination for where you are
  • Qibla direction and Sun & Moon positions
  • Saving a place to your list

Your location is used on the device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. If you place the home screen widget, the most recent coordinates are cached locally so the widget can draw itself; that cache never leaves the device either. You can revoke the permission at any time in Android Settings, and the compass keeps working.

What is stored on your device

  • Saved locations — names, notes and coordinates you choose to save, in a local database
  • Settings — theme, units, language and toggles
  • Purchase status — whether you own Pro or Remove Ads, cached so the app can honour it offline

All of it lives only on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app. We have no copy of it and no way to reach it.

Network

The app is usable with no internet connection. It makes network requests in only these cases:

  • Remote settings. The app periodically fetches a small configuration file from GitHub that controls feature switches. Like any web request, GitHub sees the IP address it came from. No identifier of you or your device is attached.
  • Purchases. Handled by Samsung's Galaxy Store, described below.
  • Advertising, if it is switched on — see the next section.

Advertising

Advertising is switched off in the current release, and no ads are served. We are telling you about it anyway because the advertising components are present in the app and can be enabled in a future release without you reinstalling.

If and when advertising is enabled, ads are served by Google AdMob, with Yandex Mobile Ads as a fallback. Those providers may collect device and usage data — including an advertising identifier — to select and measure ads, under their own policies rather than this one:

  • Google AdMob
  • Yandex

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings at any time. Purchasing Pro or Remove Ads disables advertising permanently.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Samsung Galaxy Store. Compass One never sees or stores your payment details — the app is told only whether a product is owned. Samsung's handling of the transaction is covered by Samsung's own terms and privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No crash reporting
  • No accounts, sign-in or user profiles
  • No selling or sharing of personal information — we hold none to sell
  • No advertising or tracking identifiers read by the app itself

Children

Compass One is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will look into it — though the app has no mechanism by which that could happen.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on your behalf. Everything the app stores is on your device and under your control: clear the app's data or uninstall it, and it is gone.

Permissions the app requests

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — optional, for the features listed above
  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — remote settings, purchases, and advertising if enabled
  • VIBRATE — haptic feedback when the needle crosses a cardinal direction
  • com.samsung.android.providers.context.permission.WRITE_USE_APP_FEATURE_SURVEY — required by Samsung's in-app purchase library

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes on Galaxy Store.
